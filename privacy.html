Wobblers Arena: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Wobblers Arena is a game for iPhone and for web browsers. This page explains what information the game uses and what it does with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Playing offline

Playing against bots needs no account and sends nothing to us. Your settings, such as your chosen fighter, arena and floor, are stored on your own device.

Optional account

You can create an account to keep your progress across devices. If you do, we store:

  • your username and email address;
  • your password, only as a salted hash (we cannot read it);
  • your saved game progress.

We use this only to run your account: logging in, saving your progress and recovering your password. You can change your password or delete your account from inside the game, or by emailing us. Deleting your account removes this information.

Playing online with friends

When you join or host a room, the game connects to our relay server so players in the same room can see each other. The server handles the room code, your player name, your in-game actions and, on web, any chat messages you send. This information is passed to the other players in your room and is not kept after the room closes. Like any server, it sees the network address your connection comes from while you are connected.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties.
  • We do not use your camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Children

Wobblers Arena is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
